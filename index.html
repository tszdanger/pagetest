About
I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at HKUST CSE, working with Prof. Shuai Wang. I obtained my Ph.D. from HKUST in 2025. During my Ph.D., I visited the AST Lab at ETH Zurich, advised by Prof. Zhendong Su. I received my B.S. from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. My research interests lie in Large Language Models for Code, software security, and AI safety.
