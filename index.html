Bio

About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), working with Prof. Shuai Wang. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from HKUST in 2025, where I was also advised by Prof. Shuai Wang. During my Ph.D. studies, I visited the Advanced Software Technologies (AST) Lab at ETH Zurich, advised by Prof. Zhendong Su. Prior to my Ph.D., I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen.

My research interests lie in Large Language Models for Code, software security, and AI safety.
